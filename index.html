Franchise History & Legacy

Founded in 1920 as the Decatur Staleys by George Halas, the Chicago Bears are one of the charter franchises of the National Football League (NFL). The team relocated to Chicago in 1921 and became the Bears in 1922. Known for their legendary defense and rich tradition, the Bears play home games at historic Soldier Field on Chicago's lakefront.

Hall of Fame Legends

Walter Payton

Running back "Sweetness" dominated the NFL from 1975 to 1987, winning Super Bowl XX and establishing himself as one of football's greatest players of all time.

Dick Butkus

The ultimate middle linebacker, Dick Butkus defined Chicago Bears defense with his ferocious tackling and unmatched passion on the field.

Mike Singletary

Hall of Fame linebacker and middle commander of the famed 1985 "Monsters of the Midway" defense that captured Super Bowl XX.
